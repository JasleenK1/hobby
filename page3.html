Lighting

A photo can be or can be broken by lighting. The best effects are frequently achieved with natural sunlight, although artificial lighting can be experimented with to give a dramatic effect.

Composition

Composition refers to the arrangement of elements of a photograph. One such rule is the so-called "Rule of Thirds" (when the subject is put in an off-centered position to balance the picture).

Editing

Photos are edited after being taken. The basic manipulation of brightness, contrast and color balance can significantly enhance the outcome.
