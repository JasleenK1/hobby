Photography is also a very fulfilling pastime. It gives us the opportunity to record those few moments, save some memories and communicate without using words.

    Types of Photography

    Type Description
    Landscape Concentrates on natural scenery like mountains, forests, oceans or city skylines. Frequently emphasizes the light, the weather and the expansive views.
    Portrait People centred, expression, emotion and personality centred. May be studio portraits or informal lifestyle.
    Wildlife Includes taking pictures of animals in the wild environment. Takes time, long lenses, and in most cases a lot of waiting time before you get to the right moment.
