Cameras

Photography is all about a camera. Novices usually tend to start with a DSLR or a mirrorless system. They are both manual and interchangeable-lensed, and have high quality images.

Lenses

Lenses dictate the amount of a scene that you are capturing. The wide-angle lenses are the best in landscapes and the telephonic lenses can give the close up shots in a far distance.

Accessories

  • Accessories
  • Tripod for stable shots
  • Memory cards for storage
  • External flash for lighting
